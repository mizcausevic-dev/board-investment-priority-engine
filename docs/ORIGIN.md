# Origin

Board Investment Priority Engine exists after the scorecards, trust-room, procurement-answer, RFP assembly, proof-gap, board-memo, benchmark, scenario, and allocation layers.

The earlier tools show evidence, narrative, benchmark, and allocation posture. This repo turns that stack into one board-priority surface so leadership can explain what to fund, what to protect, what to hold, what to trim, and which next ask survives a board or diligence room.
